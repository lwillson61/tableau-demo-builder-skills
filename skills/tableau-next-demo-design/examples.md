# Example — First Financial Bank digital journey

Use this as the pattern, not as a clone-by-default.

## Outcome

Digital and CX leaders see where website journeys succeed or fail, then the analytics agent names the customers to work today.

## Dashboard ahas (allowed on viz)

- High-intent completion (~58%) is the problem; check-balance (~96%) is not.
- Abandoners spend longer than completers on loan, checking, and bill pay.
- Personal loan dies at document upload; completion fell after Aug 12.

## Agent-only ahas (no viz)

- Existing customers with meaningful deposits who spent 10+ minutes on loan upload, then left, with **no open Case**.
- Upload failures concentrated on **mobile + image files**, not PDF — not a dashboard breakdown.
- Named contrast: Priya S. (abandoned, 18 min, three upload retries) vs David K. (completed, PDF first try).

## CRM actionability

- Account + Contact per named customer
- Case when they called (`Origin = Web`, subject includes journey)
- Task on loan abandoners for the CX lead
- Opportunity for personal-loan intent still sitting unsubmitted

## Agent questions (verbatim)

1. Which existing First Financial customers abandoned a personal loan at document upload after spending at least 10 minutes, and do not have an open Case?
2. For those customers, what device and file type were they using, and who should we call first based on deposit relationship?
3. Show me Priya Shah’s last session and the Salesforce follow-up we already logged.
