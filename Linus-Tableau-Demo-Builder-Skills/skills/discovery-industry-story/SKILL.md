# Discovery and Industry Story Skill

Use this skill before any demo build. It ensures the demo solves a business problem, not just a technical task.

## Outcome

Produce a crisp POV with:
- customer context,
- core business challenge,
- value hypothesis,
- prioritized use cases/personas,
- and measurable success criteria.

## Workflow

1. **Customer context**
   - Business model, growth priorities, operating constraints.
   - Current analytics maturity and decision bottlenecks.
2. **Problem framing**
   - What decision is hard today?
   - What delay/risk/cost comes from poor visibility?
3. **Value hypothesis**
   - Why Tableau Next and/or Pulse is a fit.
   - Business outcome in plain language.
4. **Use cases and personas**
   - Executive, manager, frontline analyst.
   - What each persona must see/do in the demo.
5. **Narrative design**
   - Opening KPI context -> trend -> driver breakdown -> recommended action.

## Industry-Specific Guidance

- **Financial services**
  - Common themes: growth vs risk, portfolio quality, retention, branch/channel mix.
  - Typical metrics: balances, originations, churn, delinquency, margin.
- **Insurance**
  - Common themes: loss ratio, claims leakage, underwriting quality, provider performance.
  - Typical metrics: claims volume/cost, approval cycle time, denial reasons, fraud signals.
